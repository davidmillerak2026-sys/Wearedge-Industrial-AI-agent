# SOP: Motor Drive Alarm E-07

## Scope

This SOP applies to the WearEdge Pro pilot line motor drive cabinet at station A3.

## Alarm Meaning

Alarm E-07 means the drive cabinet detected over-temperature or insufficient cooling airflow.

## Operator Reaction Plan

1. Stop feeding new parts into station A3.
2. Keep the current work order in hold state until the cabinet temperature is stable.
3. Check that the cooling fan is rotating and the inlet filter is not blocked.
4. Verify cabinet temperature on the local panel.
5. If the alarm clears after airflow recovery, restart only after maintenance confirms the filter condition.
6. If the alarm returns within 30 minutes, escalate to maintenance and do not restart the line.

## Evidence Required

- Alarm code and timestamp.
- Cabinet temperature reading.
- Fan status.
- Filter inspection result.
- Maintenance approval before release.
